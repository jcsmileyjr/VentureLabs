Our Story

Built by a Founder.
For Founders.

The Origin

I Learned the Hard Way.
You Don't Have To.

"I made every mistake in the book — and it cost me time, money, and momentum I couldn't get back."
— Founder, Midsouth Venture Labs

Midsouth Venture Labs wasn't born in a boardroom. It was born out of frustration — the kind that comes from going through a startup journey largely alone, without the right structure, the right advisors, or the right roadmap.

I built my first company the hard way. I pitched investors before my deck was ready. I set up the wrong legal structure and had to unwind it. I chased customers without a clear discovery process and wasted months building things nobody wanted. Not because I wasn't capable — but because nobody handed me a playbook, and I didn't know what I didn't know.

When I came out the other side, I made a decision: I wasn't going to let that be the story for other founders in this region. The Mid-South has extraordinary talent, grit, and ideas. What it has lacked is a dedicated home where founders can get the structured, experienced support that turns raw potential into fundable, launchable companies.

That's why I built Midsouth Venture Labs — not as a program, but as the resource I wish had existed when I was starting out.

The Problem We Solve

The Problem
Years Wasted on Avoidable Mistakes

The biggest threat to a startup isn't competition or bad timing. It's a founder spending two years learning things they could have learned in two months — wrong structure, weak pitch, no investor network, no path to customers. These aren't talent failures. They're information and access failures. And they happen every day to talented founders right here in the Mid-South.

Our Mission
Compress the Learning Curve. Accelerate the Outcome.

Midsouth Venture Labs exists to give founders the structured support, honest feedback, and warm relationships that collapse years of trial and error into a focused 12-week sprint. We don't just point you in the right direction — we walk the path with you, making sure you're set up correctly, pitching powerfully, and building a business that's ready for what comes next.

Why MVL

Deeply Local.
Fiercely Committed.

There are accelerators everywhere. Most of them are built for Silicon Valley, focused on software, and designed for founders who already have a network. Midsouth Venture Labs is different by design.

We're not a franchise of a coastal program. We're not a demo day factory. We're a locally rooted accelerator built specifically for the Mid-South ecosystem — which means we know the investors here, the corporate partners here, the customers here, and the community here in ways that no out-of-market program ever could.

When you join MVL, you're not getting a generic curriculum. You're getting a founder-to-founder relationship with someone who has been where you are, who understands the unique dynamics of building in this market, and who is personally invested in your outcome.

  • Operator Lens Coaching grounded in real startup experience, not theory — we've made the mistakes so you don't have to
