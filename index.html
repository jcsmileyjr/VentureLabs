Who We Serve

Built for Early‑Stage
Founders Ready to Build

Midsouth Venture Labs exists for early-stage founders in Memphis and across the broader Mid‑South region who have an idea, a prototype, or early traction — and need structured support to go further, faster.

We work with tech and non-tech startups alike. Whether you're building a SaaS platform, a consumer product, or a service business with scalable ambitions, our accelerator program meets you where you are.

Pre-Seed Seed Stage Tech Startups Non-Tech Startups Memphis Mid-South Region
  • You have a concept or early product but need a clear go-to-market path
  • You're preparing to raise your first or next round of funding
  • You need access to investors, customers, and corporate pilot partners
  • You want structured coaching on pitch, legal setup, and operations
  • You're committed to growing your company in or from the Mid‑South
Core Services

Five Pillars.
One Accelerator.

01
Pitch Readiness
Investor-grade pitch coaching and deck support. We sharpen your narrative, pressure-test your financials, and prepare you to walk into any room with confidence.
02
Company Setup
Legal structure, entity formation, equity splits, and operational best practices. We help you build a company that's clean for investors and built to last.
03
Investor Access
Warm introductions to angels, VCs, and family offices. A curated Demo Day that puts your company in front of the right capital — when you're ready.
04
Pilots & Customers
Structured corporate pilot programs and customer discovery frameworks. We open doors to enterprises and organizations eager to work with emerging startups.
05
Product Launch
Go-to-market strategy, launch playbooks, and hands-on support for your first (or next) product release. Turn builds into traction.
